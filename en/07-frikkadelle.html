Recipe 7

Meatballs

Spiced meatballs

Makes 12   ✦   45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 thick slice white bread
  • 125 ml milk
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 250 g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 5 ml ground coriander
  • 2 ml grated nutmeg
  • 15 ml chopped parsley
  • 7 ml salt and black pepper to taste
  • Flour for dusting
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Soak the bread in the milk, then mash it with a fork.
  2. Mix in the mince, onion, egg, spices, parsley, salt and pepper with your hands. Do not overwork it.
  3. Shape into 12 balls and flatten them slightly. Dust with flour.
  4. Fry in a little oil over medium heat for 5–6 minutes per side until cooked through. Or bake at 180 °C for 30 minutes.

Nico's tip

Serve with mashed potatoes and a tomato sauce.
