Recipe 34

Mint Sauce

Mint sauce for roast lamb

Serves 6 ✦ 10 min + 30 min standing

Ingredients

  • 125 ml finely chopped fresh mint
  • 30 ml sugar
  • 60 ml boiling water
  • 60 ml white wine vinegar
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Put the mint and sugar in a small jug.
  2. Pour the boiling water over and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Add the vinegar and salt. Leave to stand for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Nico's tip

Chop the mint just before you use it, so it stays green.
