Recipe 40

Brandy Sauce

Warm brandy butter sauce for puddings

Serves 8 ✦ 15 min

Ingredients

  • 125 g butter
  • 250 ml brown sugar
  • 250 ml cream
  • 60 ml brandy
  • 5 ml vanilla essence
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Melt the butter and sugar together over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the cream and simmer for 5 minutes, until slightly thick.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the brandy, vanilla and salt.
  4. Serve warm over baked puddings, pancakes or ice cream.

Nico's tip

Add the brandy off the heat, so it keeps its flavour.
