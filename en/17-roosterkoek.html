Recipe 42

Braai Bread Rolls

Bread rolls baked on the braai grid

Makes 16   ✦   2 h including rising

Ingredients

  • 1 kg cake or bread flour
  • 10 g instant dry yeast
  • 15 ml sugar
  • 10 ml salt
  • 30 ml oil
  • About 650 ml lukewarm water

Method

  1. Mix the dry ingredients. Add the oil and enough water to form a soft dough. Knead for 10 minutes.
  2. Cover and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
  3. Divide into 16 pieces, shape into balls and flatten slightly. Rise for 20 minutes.
  4. Bake on a grid over low coals for 15–20 minutes, turning often. They are ready when they sound hollow when tapped.

Nico's tip

The coals must be low; you should be able to hold your hand over them for a count of ten.
