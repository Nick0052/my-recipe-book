Recipe 49

Ginger Biscuits

Crisp ginger biscuits

Makes about 48 ✦ 45 min

Ingredients

  • 750 ml cake flour
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 20 ml ground ginger
  • 5 ml ground cinnamon
  • 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 ml salt
  • 125 g butter
  • 125 ml golden syrup
  • 1 egg

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease two baking trays.
  2. Melt the butter and syrup together over low heat. Let it cool slightly.
  3. Sift the flour, ginger, cinnamon, bicarbonate of soda and salt. Stir in the sugar.
  4. Add the butter mixture and the beaten egg and mix to a firm dough.
  5. Roll into balls the size of a walnut, place them 5 cm apart on the trays and press them flat with a fork.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until golden brown. Let them firm up on the tray for a few minutes, then cool on a rack.

Nico's tip

They crisp up as they cool.
