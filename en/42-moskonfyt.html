Recipe 59

Grape Must Syrup

Grape-must syrup

Makes about 2 small jars ✦ 2 h

Ingredients

  • 2 litres freshly pressed grape juice (or unsweetened pure grape juice)

Method

  1. Pour the juice into a wide heavy pot and bring it to the boil. Skim off the foam.
  2. Boil gently, uncovered, for 1½–2 hours, skimming now and then, until it has reduced to about a third and is as thick as thin syrup.
  3. It thickens more as it cools: test a few drops on a cold saucer.
  4. Pour into hot, sterilised jars and seal.

Nico's tip

Eat it on aniseed buns or fresh bread with butter.
