Recipe 22

Tomato and Onion Salad

Diced tomato and onion in the salad dressing of your choice

Serves 6 ✦ 10 min

Ingredients

  • 6 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 60 ml salad dressing of your choice
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Dice the tomatoes and the onion into small cubes of about the same size.
  2. Put them in a bowl and pour the salad dressing over.
  3. Mix gently, season with salt and pepper and serve.

Nico's tip

Make it just before serving; tomatoes go watery if they stand.
