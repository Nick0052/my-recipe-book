Recipe 6

Chicken Pie

Chicken pie with egg and spices

Serves 6   ✦   2 h

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken pieces
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 bay leaves, 6 peppercorns, 4 allspice berries (wonderpeper), 3 cloves
  • 45 ml butter
  • 45 ml flour
  • 500 ml chicken stock (from cooking the chicken)
  • 2 ml grated nutmeg
  • 1 egg yolk, plus 1 egg for glazing
  • 30 ml lemon juice
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • 125 g ham, diced (optional)
  • 400 g puff pastry
  • Salt and white pepper

Method

  1. Cover the chicken, onion and whole spices with water. Simmer for 1 hour until tender. Strain and keep 500 ml of the stock.
  2. Remove the skin and bones and cut the meat into pieces.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the stock and stir until thick. Add the nutmeg and season.
  4. Take the sauce off the heat. Beat the egg yolk with the lemon juice and stir it in.
  5. Mix in the chicken and ham. Spoon half into a pie dish, add the egg slices, then the rest of the filling. Let it cool.
  6. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cover with the pastry, cut a steam hole and brush with beaten egg.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until puffed and golden.

Nico's tip

The allspice gives it the true old-fashioned flavour.
