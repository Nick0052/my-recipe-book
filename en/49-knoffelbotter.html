Recipe 32

Garlic Butter

Garlic and parsley butter

Makes about 250 g ✦ 10 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g butter, soft
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 30 ml chopped parsley
  • 15 ml lemon juice
  • 2 ml salt
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Mash everything together with a fork until evenly mixed.
  2. Spoon onto a sheet of baking paper, roll it into a log and twist the ends.
  3. Chill until firm. Slice off rounds as you need them.

Nico's tip

It freezes well for three months.
