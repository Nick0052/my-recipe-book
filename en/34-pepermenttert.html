Recipe 51

Peppermint Crisp Tart

No-bake fridge tart with caramel and peppermint chocolate

Serves 10 ✦ 30 min + 4 h chilling

Ingredients

  • 400 g coconut tea biscuits (2 packets Tennis biscuits)
  • 500 ml whipping cream
  • 360 g tinned caramel (1 tin Caramel Treat)
  • 150 g peppermint chocolate (3 Peppermint Crisp bars), grated

Method

  1. Whip the cream until stiff.
  2. Stir the caramel until smooth, then fold it into the cream with two thirds of the grated chocolate.
  3. Cover the base of a 30 × 20 cm dish with a layer of whole biscuits.
  4. Spread a third of the caramel cream over the biscuits. Repeat the layers twice more, ending with cream.
  5. Sprinkle the rest of the chocolate over the top.
  6. Cover and chill for at least 4 hours, or overnight, until the biscuits have softened.

Nico's tip

Chill the chocolate first; it grates much more easily.
