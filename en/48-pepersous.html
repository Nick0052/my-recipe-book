Recipe 31

Pepper Sauce

Green peppercorn and brandy cream sauce

Serves 4 ✦ 15 min

Ingredients

  • 30 ml butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 30 ml green peppercorns in brine, drained
  • 30 ml brandy
  • 125 ml beef stock
  • 250 ml cream
  • 5 ml prepared mustard
  • Salt

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the butter until soft. Add the peppercorns and crush a few with the back of a spoon.
  2. Add the brandy and let it bubble for a minute.
  3. Add the stock and boil until reduced by half.
  4. Stir in the cream and mustard and simmer for 5 minutes, until thick. Season with salt.

Nico's tip

Make it in the pan the steak was fried in.
