Recipe 27

Apricot Glaze

Apricot basting sauce for snoek and gammon

Enough for 1 snoek ✦ 10 min

Ingredients

  • 125 ml apricot jam
  • 60 g butter
  • 30 ml lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Melt the jam, butter, lemon juice and garlic together over low heat. Season.
  2. Braai the snoek skin side down and brush it with the sauce every few minutes.
  3. For gammon, brush it over the scored fat for the last 20 minutes in the oven.

Nico's tip

Add a little chilli for some bite.
