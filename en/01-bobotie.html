Recipe 1

Bobotie

Spiced mince baked under an egg custard

Serves 6   ✦   1 h 15 min

Ingredients

  • 30 ml oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 30 ml medium curry powder
  • 5 ml turmeric
  • 5 ml ground coriander
  • 1 kg beef mince
  • 2 slices white bread, crusts removed
  • 375 ml milk
  • 45 ml fruit chutney
  • 30 ml apricot jam
  • 30 ml lemon juice
  • 60 ml sultanas
  • 10 ml salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 bay leaves

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a 30 × 20 cm oven dish.
  2. Fry the onions in the oil until soft and golden. Add the garlic, curry powder, turmeric and coriander and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Add the mince and fry, breaking it up, until it is no longer pink.
  4. Soak the bread in the milk, then squeeze it out and keep the milk. Mash the bread into the mince with the chutney, jam, lemon juice, sultanas, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon the mixture into the dish and press it level.
  6. Beat the eggs into the reserved milk (top up to 250 ml if needed) and pour it over the mince. Push the bay leaves in upright.
  7. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the custard is set and golden.

Nico's tip

Serve with yellow rice, chutney and sliced banana.
