Recipe 17

Mustard Onions

Baby onions in a sweet-sour mustard sauce

Serves 6–8 ✦ 40 min + chilling

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pickling onions, peeled
  • 2 eggs
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 15 ml mustard powder
  • 5 ml salt
  • 125 ml brown vinegar
  • 125 ml water
  • 60 ml cream (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the onions in salted water for 10–15 minutes, until just tender but still whole. Drain well.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, mustard powder and salt in a heatproof bowl. Whisk in the vinegar and water.
  3. Set the bowl over a pot of simmering water and stir for 8–10 minutes, until the sauce is thick enough to coat a spoon. Do not let it boil.
  4. Take it off the heat and stir in the cream, if using.
  5. Pour the sauce over the onions. Cool, then chill for at least 2 hours.

Nico's tip

Even better the next day, served cold with braaivleis.
