Recipe 64

Pickled Beetroot

Sweet-sour bottled beetroot

Makes about 3 jars ✦ 1 h 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beetroot
  • 1 onion, sliced into thin rings
  • 375 ml brown vinegar
  • 125 ml water
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 5 ml salt
  • 4 whole cloves

Method

  1. Boil the beetroot whole, in their skins, for 40–50 minutes, until tender. Cool, rub off the skins and slice or dice.
  2. Bring the vinegar, water, sugar, salt and cloves to the boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Add the beetroot and onion and boil for 5 minutes.
  4. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars, cover with the liquid and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Leave a bit of stalk on while it boils, so the colour does not bleed.
