Recipe 28

Peri-Peri Sauce

Chilli, garlic and lemon sauce

Makes about 250 ml ✦ 20 min

Ingredients

  • 8 red bird's-eye (peri-peri) chillies, stalks removed
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 125 ml oil
  • 60 ml lemon juice
  • 30 ml red wine vinegar
  • 10 ml paprika
  • 5 ml dried oregano
  • 5 ml salt
  • 2 bay leaves

Method

  1. Blend the chillies, garlic, lemon juice, vinegar, paprika, oregano and salt until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil gently in a small pot with the bay leaves. Add the chilli mixture.
  3. Simmer over very low heat for 10 minutes, stirring. Cool and remove the bay leaves.
  4. Pour into a clean bottle and keep in the fridge. Shake before using.

Nico's tip

Take the seeds out of half the chillies for a milder sauce.
