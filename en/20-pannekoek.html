Recipe 47

Pancakes

Thin pancakes with cinnamon sugar

Makes about 16   ✦   45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g (500 ml) cake flour
  • 2 ml salt
  • 3 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 125 ml water
  • 60 ml oil, plus extra for the pan
  • 15 ml brandy (optional)
  • 125 ml sugar mixed with 10 ml ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon, for squeezing

Method

  1. Sift the flour and salt. Whisk the eggs, milk, water, oil and brandy.
  2. Beat the liquid into the flour to make a thin, smooth batter. Rest it for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled pan. Pour in just enough batter to coat the base thinly.
  4. Cook until the edges lift, then turn and cook the other side for a few seconds.
  5. Sprinkle with cinnamon sugar and a little lemon juice. Roll up and keep warm.

Nico's tip

Pancakes belong to a rainy day.
