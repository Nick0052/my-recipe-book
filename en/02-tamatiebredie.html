Recipe 2

Tomato Bredie

Lamb and tomato stew

Serves 6   ✦   2 h 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg lamb neck or shoulder chops
  • 30 ml oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 green chilli, chopped (optional)
  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, skinned and chopped (or 2 × 400 g tins)
  • 30 ml tomato paste
  • 15 ml sugar
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in a heavy pot, in batches. Set aside.
  2. Fry the onions in the same pot until soft. Add the garlic, cinnamon, cloves and chilli and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Return the meat. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste and sugar and season well.
  4. Cover and simmer very gently for 1½ hours, stirring now and then.
  5. Add the potatoes and cook for 30–40 minutes more, uncovered, until the sauce is thick and the meat falls off the bone.

Nico's tip

A bredie is always better the next day.
