Recipe 33

Rooibos Butter

Compound butter with rooibos, honey and orange

Makes about 250 g ✦ 20 min + 1 h chilling

Ingredients

  • 250 g butter, soft
  • 2 rooibos teabags
  • 60 ml boiling water
  • 30 ml honey
  • 5 ml finely grated orange zest
  • 2 ml salt (leave out if the butter is salted)

Method

  1. Steep one teabag in the boiling water for 10 minutes, then squeeze the bag out well.
  2. Simmer the tea with the honey in a small pot until it has reduced to about 15 ml of syrup. Cool completely.
  3. Cut open the second teabag and grind the dry leaves to a fine powder with a pestle and mortar. Sift out any woody bits.
  4. Beat the butter until pale and creamy. Beat in the cooled syrup a little at a time, so it blends in and does not separate.
  5. Mix in the ground rooibos, orange zest and salt.
  6. Spoon onto baking paper, roll into a log, twist the ends and chill for at least 1 hour.
  7. For a savoury braai butter, leave out the honey and orange zest and add 1 crushed garlic clove, 5 ml smoked paprika, 5 ml lemon zest and black pepper.

Nico's tip

The syrup must be cold before it goes into the butter, or the butter splits.
