Recipe 56

Koeksisters

Plaited doughnuts in ice-cold syrup

Makes about 30   ✦   2 h + overnight chilling

Ingredients

  • Syrup (make a day ahead):
  • 1 kg sugar
  • 500 ml water
  • 2 ml cream of tartar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 cm piece fresh ginger, sliced
  • 15 ml lemon juice
  • Dough:
  • 500 g cake flour
  • 25 ml baking powder
  • 2 ml salt
  • 50 g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 250 ml buttermilk or milk
  • Oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Syrup: Heat the sugar and water, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Add the other syrup ingredients and boil for 7 minutes without stirring. Cool, then chill overnight.
  2. Dough: Sift the flour, baking powder and salt. Rub in the butter. Beat the egg with the buttermilk and mix in to make a soft dough. Cover and rest for 1 hour.
  3. Roll the dough out 5 mm thick. Cut into 8 × 3 cm rectangles. Cut each into three strands, joined at the top.
  4. Plait the strands and pinch the ends together firmly.
  5. Divide the syrup between two bowls. Keep one in the freezer while you use the other, and swap them.
  6. Fry in oil at 180 °C for 1½–2 minutes, turning once, until deep golden.
  7. Drain for a few seconds, then plunge straight into the ice-cold syrup for 30 seconds. Lift out onto a rack.

Nico's tip

Hot koeksister, ice-cold syrup. That's the whole secret.
