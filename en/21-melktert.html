Recipe 50

Milk Tart

Milk tart with cinnamon

One 23 cm tart   ✦   1 h + 2 h setting

Ingredients

  • Crust:
  • 125 g butter, soft
  • 100 ml sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 375 ml cake flour
  • 5 ml baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • Filling:
  • 1 litre milk
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 30 g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 150 ml sugar
  • 60 ml cornflour
  • 30 ml cake flour
  • 2 ml salt
  • 5 ml vanilla essence
  • Ground cinnamon for dusting

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the egg, then mix in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  2. Press the dough into a greased 23 cm pie dish. Prick the base and bake for 15 minutes until golden. Cool.
  3. Heat the milk with the cinnamon stick and butter until just boiling.
  4. Whisk the eggs, sugar, cornflour, flour and salt. Whisk in a cup of the hot milk, then pour it all back into the pot.
  5. Stir over medium heat for 5 minutes, until thick and smooth. Remove the cinnamon stick and add the vanilla.
  6. Pour into the crust, dust with cinnamon and leave to set for at least 2 hours.

Nico's tip

Stir all the time, or the milk will catch.
