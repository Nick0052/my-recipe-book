Recipe 21

Tomato Sauce Pasta Salad

Cold pasta salad in a sweet-sour tomato-sauce dressing

Serves 8–10 ✦ 30 min + chilling

Ingredients

  • 500 g screw noodles (fusilli)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green pepper, finely chopped
  • 250 ml tomato sauce
  • 125 ml oil
  • 125 ml brown vinegar
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 10 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 5 ml salt
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the noodles in salted water until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water.
  2. Whisk the tomato sauce, oil, vinegar, sugar, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Mix the noodles, onion and green pepper in a large bowl.
  4. Pour the dressing over and toss well.
  5. Cover and chill for at least 4 hours, or overnight, stirring once or twice.

Nico's tip

Make it the day before; the noodles drink up the dressing.
