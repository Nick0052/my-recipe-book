Recipe 25

Braai Spice

Spice mix for the braai

Makes about 200 ml ✦ 10 min

Ingredients

  • 60 ml coarse salt
  • 30 ml whole coriander seeds
  • 15 ml black peppercorns
  • 30 ml paprika
  • 15 ml brown sugar
  • 10 ml garlic powder
  • 10 ml onion powder
  • 5 ml dried thyme
  • 2 ml ground cloves

Method

  1. Toast the coriander seeds and peppercorns in a dry pan for 1–2 minutes, until fragrant.
  2. Crush them coarsely with a pestle and mortar.
  3. Mix with all the other ingredients.
  4. Store in an airtight jar. Rub it onto the meat just before it goes on the fire.

Nico's tip

Toasted coriander is the taste of boerewors and biltong.
