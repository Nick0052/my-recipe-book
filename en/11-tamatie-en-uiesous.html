Recipe 12

Tomato and Onion Sauce

Tomato and onion relish (sheba)

Serves 6   ✦   35 min

Ingredients

  • 30 ml oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 1 green chilli, chopped (optional)
  • 6 ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 2 × 400 g tins)
  • 15 ml sugar
  • 15 ml vinegar
  • 15 ml chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Fry the onions in the oil until soft. Add the garlic, green pepper and chilli and fry for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes, sugar and vinegar. Season well.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes, until thick. Stir in the parsley.

Nico's tip

Serve hot over pap, with boerewors.
