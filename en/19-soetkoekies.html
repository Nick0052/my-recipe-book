Recipe 48

Old-Fashioned Spice Biscuits

Old-fashioned spiced wine biscuits

Makes about 60   ✦   1 h 30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cake flour
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 10 ml ground ginger
  • 10 ml ground cinnamon
  • 5 ml ground cloves
  • 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 ml salt
  • 125 g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 60 ml sweet red wine (or milk)

Method

  1. Sift the flour, spices, bicarbonate of soda and salt. Stir in the sugar.
  2. Rub in the butter.
  3. Beat the eggs with the wine and mix in to form a stiff dough. Cover and rest for 1 hour.
  4. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Roll the dough out 5 mm thick and cut out shapes.
  5. Bake on greased trays for 10–12 minutes, until just firm. Cool on a rack.

Nico's tip

Store in a sealed tin; they improve after a few days.
