Recipe 61

Peach Chutney

Yellow peach chutney

Makes about 4 jars ✦ 1 h 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg yellow cling peaches, peeled, stoned and chopped
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 250 ml seedless raisins
  • 500 ml brown vinegar
  • 375 ml sugar
  • 10 ml ground ginger
  • 5 ml ground coriander
  • 5 ml cayenne pepper (or to taste)
  • 10 ml salt

Method

  1. Put everything into a large heavy pot.
  2. Bring slowly to the boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Simmer uncovered for about 1 hour, stirring often, until thick and glossy.
  4. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Firm, slightly under-ripe peaches keep their shape best.
