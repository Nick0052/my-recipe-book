Recipe 19

Potato Salad

Potato salad with egg and a creamy dressing

Serves 6–8 ✦ 40 min + chilling

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 30 ml chopped parsley
  • 250 ml condensed milk mayonnaise (or plain mayonnaise)
  • 5 ml salt and black pepper to taste
  • Paprika for dusting

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins in salted water for 20–25 minutes, until just tender. Drain and cool slightly.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes.
  3. Mix the potatoes, eggs, onion and parsley in a large bowl.
  4. Fold in the dressing while the potatoes are still warm, so they soak it up. Season.
  5. Chill for at least 1 hour. Dust with paprika before serving.

Nico's tip

Waxy potatoes hold their shape; floury ones fall apart.
