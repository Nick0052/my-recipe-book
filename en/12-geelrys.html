Recipe 14

Yellow Rice

Yellow rice with raisins

Serves 6   ✦   30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml long-grain white rice
  • 1.25 litres water
  • 5 ml turmeric
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 5 ml salt
  • 45 ml sugar
  • 125 ml seedless raisins
  • 30 ml butter

Method

  1. Put everything except the butter into a pot and bring it to the boil.
  2. Turn the heat down, cover and simmer for 20 minutes until the rice is tender.
  3. Remove the cinnamon stick, stir in the butter and fluff with a fork.

Nico's tip

Yellow rice and bobotie belong together.
