Recipe 8

Sosaties

Curried lamb and apricot kebabs

Makes 12 skewers   ✦   30 min + 24 h marinating

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg boneless leg of lamb, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 200 g dried apricots
  • 2 onions, cut into chunks
  • 12 skewers
  • Marinade:
  • 30 ml oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 30 ml medium curry powder
  • 5 ml turmeric
  • 250 ml brown vinegar
  • 125 ml water
  • 60 ml apricot jam
  • 30 ml brown sugar
  • 4 bay or lemon leaves
  • 10 ml salt

Method

  1. Fry the chopped onion and garlic in the oil until soft. Add the curry powder and turmeric and fry for 1 minute.
  2. Add the vinegar, water, jam, sugar, leaves and salt. Simmer for 5 minutes, then cool completely.
  3. Pour over the lamb in a glass or ceramic dish. Cover and refrigerate for 24–48 hours, turning now and then.
  4. Thread the lamb onto skewers, alternating with apricots and onion.
  5. Braai over medium coals for 12–15 minutes, turning often.
  6. Boil the leftover marinade for 5 minutes and thicken it with 15 ml cornflour to serve as a sauce.

Nico's tip

The longer it marinates, the more tender the meat.
