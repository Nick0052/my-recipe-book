Recipe 29

Monkey Gland Sauce

Sweet and tangy steakhouse sauce

Makes about 500 ml ✦ 30 min

Ingredients

  • 15 ml oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 250 ml tomato sauce
  • 125 ml fruit chutney
  • 60 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 30 ml brown vinegar
  • 30 ml brown sugar
  • 125 ml water
  • A few drops of Tabasco (optional)

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft. Add the garlic and fry for 1 minute.
  2. Add all the other ingredients and stir well.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
  4. Serve hot over steak, burgers or ribs.

Nico's tip

Despite the name, there is no monkey in it.
