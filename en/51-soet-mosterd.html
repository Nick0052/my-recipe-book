Recipe 35

Sweet Mustard

Cooked sweet mustard

Makes about 400 ml ✦ 20 min

Ingredients

  • 60 ml mustard powder
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 15 ml cake flour
  • 2 ml salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 125 ml white vinegar
  • 125 ml water

Method

  1. Mix the mustard powder, sugar, flour and salt in a small heavy pot.
  2. Whisk in the eggs, then the vinegar and water, until smooth.
  3. Stir over low heat for 8–10 minutes, until it thickens. Do not let it boil.
  4. Pour into clean jars, cool and keep in the fridge.

Nico's tip

Serve with cold meat, tongue or gammon.
