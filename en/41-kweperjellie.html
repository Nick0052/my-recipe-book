Recipe 58

Quince Jelly

Clear quince jelly

Makes about 3 jars ✦ 2 h + overnight draining

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg quinces, washed and chopped (skin and cores included)
  • 2 litres water
  • Sugar (250 ml for every 250 ml of juice)
  • 30 ml lemon juice

Method

  1. Simmer the quinces in the water for about 1 hour, until very soft and pink.
  2. Pour into a muslin-lined sieve over a bowl and let it drip overnight. Do not squeeze, or the jelly turns cloudy.
  3. Measure the juice. Put it in a clean pot with the same volume of sugar and the lemon juice.
  4. Stir over low heat until the sugar dissolves, then boil rapidly for 15–25 minutes, skimming off the foam.
  5. Test on a cold saucer: if it wrinkles when you push it, it is ready.
  6. Pour into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Good with roast lamb and with cheese.
