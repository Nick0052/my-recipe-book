Sweet-and-Sour Cucumber Salad

Thin cucumber slices in sweetened vinegar

Serves 6 ✦ 15 min + 1 h standing

Ingredients

  • 2 cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced into thin rings
  • 10 ml salt
  • 125 ml white vinegar
  • 60 ml sugar
  • 60 ml water
  • 15 ml chopped dill (optional)

Method

  1. Sprinkle the cucumber and onion with the salt and leave in a colander for 30 minutes. Squeeze out the liquid.
  2. Stir the vinegar, sugar and water together until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pour over the cucumber and onion and add the dill.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Nico's tip

It keeps for three days in the fridge.
