Recipe 54

Cape Brandy Pudding

Cape brandy pudding with dates

Serves 8   ✦   1 h 15 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g pitted dates, chopped
  • 250 ml boiling water
  • 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
  • 125 g butter
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 500 ml cake flour
  • 5 ml baking powder
  • 2 ml salt
  • 125 ml pecan nuts or walnuts, chopped
  • Syrup:
  • 375 ml sugar
  • 250 ml water
  • 15 ml butter
  • 5 ml vanilla essence
  • A pinch of salt
  • 125 ml brandy

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a 25 cm oven dish.
  2. Pour the boiling water over the dates, stir in the bicarbonate of soda and leave to cool.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  4. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt. Fold into the butter with the date mixture and nuts.
  5. Pour into the dish and bake for 40–45 minutes.
  6. While it bakes, boil the sugar, water and butter for 5 minutes. Remove from the heat and add the vanilla, salt and brandy.
  7. Pour the hot syrup over the pudding as it comes out of the oven. Let it soak in for 15 minutes.

Nico's tip

Serve with thick cream.
