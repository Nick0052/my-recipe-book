Recipe 18

Curried Green Beans

Curried green bean preserve

Makes about 4 jars   ✦   45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg green beans, sliced
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 375 ml brown vinegar
  • 200 ml sugar
  • 10 ml salt
  • 15 ml medium curry powder
  • 5 ml turmeric
  • 20 ml cornflour

Method

  1. Cook the beans in salted water for 10 minutes. Drain.
  2. Simmer the onions in the vinegar with the sugar and salt for 10 minutes.
  3. Mix the curry powder, turmeric and cornflour with a little cold water. Stir it into the onions and cook until thick.
  4. Add the beans and boil for 5 minutes.
  5. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Keep the jars in a cool cupboard. They keep for months.
