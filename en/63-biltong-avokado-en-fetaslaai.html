Recipe 24

Biltong, Avocado and Feta Salad

Green salad with biltong, avocado and feta

Serves 4–6 ✦ 15 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g mixed salad leaves
  • 2 ripe avocados, sliced
  • 100 g biltong, thinly sliced
  • 100 g feta, crumbled
  • 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 60 ml olive oil
  • 30 ml lemon juice
  • 5 ml honey
  • 5 ml prepared mustard
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Whisk the oil, lemon juice, honey and mustard together. Season.
  2. Arrange the leaves on a platter and scatter the tomatoes over.
  3. Top with the avocado, biltong and feta.
  4. Drizzle the dressing over just before serving.

Nico's tip

Slice the avocado last and squeeze lemon over it, so it does not go brown.
