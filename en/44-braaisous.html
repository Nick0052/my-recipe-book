Recipe 26

Braai Sauce

Marinade and basting sauce for the braai

Makes about 350 ml ✦ 20 min

Ingredients

  • 125 ml tomato sauce
  • 60 ml fruit chutney
  • 60 ml oil
  • 60 ml brown vinegar
  • 30 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 30 ml brown sugar
  • 15 ml prepared mustard
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 5 ml salt

Method

  1. Put everything in a small pot and bring to a simmer.
  2. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring, then cool.
  3. Marinate chops, chicken or ribs in half the sauce for at least 2 hours.
  4. Baste with the rest during the last 10 minutes of the braai, so the sugar does not burn.

Nico's tip

Keeps for two weeks in the fridge.
