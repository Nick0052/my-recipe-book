Recipe 10

Skilpadjies

Lamb's liver wrapped in caul fat, braaied over coals

Makes about 12 ✦ 45 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb's liver, membrane removed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 15 ml oil
  • 10 ml ground coriander
  • 2 ml ground cloves
  • 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 15 ml brown vinegar
  • 5 ml salt and black pepper to taste
  • 250 g caul fat (netvet), soaked in lukewarm water

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft. Let it cool.
  2. Chop the liver finely, or mince it coarsely. Mix with the onion, coriander, cloves, Worcestershire sauce, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the caul fat out on a board and cut it into squares of about 15 cm.
  4. Put a heaped spoonful of liver on each square and fold the fat around it into a neat parcel. Secure with a toothpick if needed.
  5. Braai over medium coals for 10–15 minutes, turning often, until the fat is crisp and golden and the liver is just cooked.

Nico's tip

Serve hot off the grid with pap and tomato and onion sauce.
