Recipe 4

Chicken Potjie

Chicken potjiekos over coals

Serves 6–8   ✦   2 h 30 min   ✦   No. 3 potjie

Ingredients

  • 30 ml oil
  • 250 g streaky bacon, chopped
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1.8 kg chicken pieces (thighs and drumsticks)
  • 10 ml paprika
  • 5 ml dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 125 ml dry white wine
  • 250 ml chicken stock
  • 4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 6 medium potatoes, halved
  • 250 g button mushrooms
  • 4 baby marrows, sliced
  • 125 ml cream (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the potjie over a medium fire. Fry the bacon in the oil until crisp, then add the onions and garlic and fry until soft.
  2. Add the chicken and brown on all sides. Season with paprika, thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the bay leaves, wine and stock. Put the lid on and cook over a few coals for 45 minutes.
  4. Layer the vegetables on top in this order: carrots, potatoes, mushrooms, baby marrows. Season each layer. Do not stir.
  5. Cover and simmer over low coals for 1–1½ hours. Add a little stock if it cooks dry.
  6. Pour in the cream for the last 10 minutes, if using.

Nico's tip

Don't stir! The layers cook in their own steam.
