Recipe 43

Ash Bread

Bread baked in the ashes

Makes 8 ✦ 1 h 45 min including rising

Ingredients

  • 500 g white bread flour
  • 5 g instant dry yeast
  • 5 ml sugar
  • 5 ml salt
  • 15 ml oil
  • About 300 ml lukewarm water
  • Extra flour for dusting

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the oil and enough water to form a firm dough. Knead for 10 minutes.
  2. Cover and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
  3. Divide into 8 pieces and shape into flat cakes about 2 cm thick. Dust them well with flour.
  4. Rake the coals aside and lay the cakes straight on the hot ash. Cover them with more hot ash.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes, turning once. They are ready when they sound hollow when tapped.
  6. Knock and brush off the ash and break them open while hot.

Nico's tip

Use ash from a wood fire, not from briquettes.
