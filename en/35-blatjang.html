Recipe 60

Fruit Chutney

Fruit chutney with dried apricots

Makes about 4 jars ✦ 1 h 30 min + overnight soaking

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried apricots, chopped
  • 250 g seedless raisins
  • 750 ml brown vinegar
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 375 ml brown sugar
  • 10 ml ground ginger
  • 10 ml ground coriander
  • 5 ml cayenne pepper (or to taste)
  • 10 ml salt

Method

  1. Soak the apricots and raisins in the vinegar overnight (or for at least 4 hours).
  2. Put the fruit and vinegar in a heavy pot with all the other ingredients.
  3. Bring slowly to the boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Simmer uncovered for 1–1½ hours, stirring often, until thick and glossy.
  5. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Let it stand for two weeks before opening; the flavour mellows.
