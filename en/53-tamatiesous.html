Recipe 37

Homemade Tomato Sauce

Old-fashioned bottled tomato sauce

Makes about 1 litre ✦ 2 h

Ingredients

  • 2 kg ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 250 ml brown vinegar
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 15 ml salt
  • 5 ml ground ginger
  • 5 ml ground allspice
  • 2 ml cayenne pepper
  • 4 whole cloves

Method

  1. Simmer the tomatoes, onions and garlic in a large heavy pot for 30 minutes, until very soft.
  2. Push the mixture through a sieve and return the purée to the pot.
  3. Add the vinegar, sugar, salt and spices. Simmer uncovered for about 1 hour, stirring often, until thick.
  4. Remove the cloves. Pour into hot, sterilised bottles and seal at once.

Nico's tip

It thickens more as it cools.
