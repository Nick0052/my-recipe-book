Recipe 38

Chakalaka

Spicy vegetable relish

Serves 6–8 ✦ 40 min

Ingredients

  • 30 ml oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 15 ml medium curry powder
  • 3 carrots, grated
  • 400 g tinned chopped tomatoes (1 tin)
  • 410 g tinned baked beans in tomato sauce (1 tin)
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft. Add the garlic, green pepper and chillies and fry for 3 minutes.
  2. Stir in the curry powder and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Add the carrots and tomatoes. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, until the carrots are soft.
  4. Add the beans, season and heat through. Serve warm or cold.

Nico's tip

Even better the next day with pap and wors.
