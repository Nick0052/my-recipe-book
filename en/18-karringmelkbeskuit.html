Recipe 45

Buttermilk Rusks

Buttermilk rusks

Makes about 60   ✦   1 h 30 min + overnight drying

Ingredients

  • 1 kg self-raising flour
  • 10 ml baking powder
  • 5 ml salt
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 250 g butter, cold and cubed
  • 500 ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Grease two loaf tins or one 30 × 23 cm tin.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt. Stir in the sugar, then rub in the butter until it looks like breadcrumbs.
  3. Whisk the buttermilk and eggs. Mix into the flour to form a soft dough.
  4. Roll into golf-ball-sized balls and pack them tightly into the tins.
  5. Bake for 1 hour, until golden and a skewer comes out clean. Cool.
  6. Break the balls apart (or cut into fingers). Dry at 90–100 °C for 6 hours or overnight, with the oven door slightly open.

Nico's tip

Dunk in a mug of coffee.
