Recipe 62

Atchar

Mixed vegetables pickled in spiced oil

Makes about 3 jars ✦ 45 min + overnight salting

Ingredients

  • 250 g green beans, sliced
  • 250 g cauliflower, in small florets
  • 2 carrots, cut into thin sticks
  • 250 g pickling onions, peeled
  • 60 ml coarse salt
  • 375 ml oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 15 ml medium curry powder
  • 10 ml turmeric
  • 5 ml fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 125 ml white vinegar

Method

  1. Mix the vegetables with the salt, cover with cold water and leave overnight. Drain and rinse well.
  2. Blanch the vegetables in boiling water for 2 minutes. Drain and let them dry completely on a clean cloth.
  3. Heat the oil gently and fry the garlic, curry powder, turmeric, fenugreek and chillies for 1 minute. Take off the heat, add the vinegar and cool.
  4. Pack the vegetables into sterilised jars and pour the spiced oil over so they are completely covered.
  5. Seal and leave for at least a week before eating.

Nico's tip

Keep the vegetables under the oil, or they spoil.
