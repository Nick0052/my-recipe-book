Recipe 3

Waterblommetjie Bredie

Lamb stew with Cape pondweed flowers

Serves 6   ✦   2 h

Ingredients

  • 750 g fresh waterblommetjies (or 2 × 400 g tins, drained)
  • 1.5 kg lamb rib or neck, cut into pieces
  • 30 ml oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 250 ml water or lamb stock
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 5 ml grated nutmeg
  • A handful of wild sorrel (suring), or the juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and white pepper

Method

  1. Soak fresh waterblommetjies overnight in salted water. Rinse well and trim the stalks.
  2. Brown the lamb in the oil in a heavy pot. Add the onions and fry until soft.
  3. Add the water, season, cover and simmer gently for 1 hour.
  4. Layer the potatoes and then the waterblommetjies on top of the meat. Sprinkle with nutmeg.
  5. Cover and simmer for 30–40 minutes. Do not stir: shake the pot gently instead.
  6. Add the sorrel or lemon juice for the last 10 minutes.

Nico's tip

Fresh waterblommetjies are only in season in winter and spring.
