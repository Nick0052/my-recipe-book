Recipe 55

Cinnamon Dumplings

Cinnamon dumplings in syrup

Serves 6   ✦   30 min

Ingredients

  • Dumplings:
  • 250 ml cake flour
  • 10 ml baking powder
  • 1 ml salt
  • 15 ml butter
  • 1 egg
  • 125 ml milk
  • Syrup:
  • 500 ml water
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 30 ml butter
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • To serve: 60 ml sugar mixed with 5 ml ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Bring the syrup ingredients to a simmer in a wide pot with a lid.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt. Rub in the butter.
  3. Beat the egg and milk and stir into the flour to make a thick batter.
  4. Drop spoonfuls into the simmering syrup, leaving room between them.
  5. Cover and simmer for 12 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  6. Serve hot with the syrup, sprinkled with cinnamon sugar.

Nico's tip

Lift the lid and the dumplings go flat.
