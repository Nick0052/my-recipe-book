Recipe 57

Apricot Jam

Homemade apricot jam

Makes about 4 jars ✦ 1 h

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe but firm apricots, halved and stoned
  • 125 ml water
  • 750 g sugar
  • 30 ml lemon juice

Method

  1. Put a saucer in the freezer for testing.
  2. Simmer the apricots in the water in a large heavy pot for 10–15 minutes, until soft.
  3. Add the sugar and lemon juice and stir over low heat until the sugar has dissolved completely.
  4. Boil rapidly for 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then and skimming off the foam.
  5. Test: drop a little jam on the cold saucer. If it wrinkles when you push it with a finger, it is ready.
  6. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Slightly under-ripe fruit sets best.
