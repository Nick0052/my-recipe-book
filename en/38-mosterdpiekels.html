Recipe 63

Mustard Pickles

Vegetables in a sweet mustard sauce (piccalilli)

Makes about 4 jars ✦ 1 h + overnight salting

Ingredients

  • 500 g cauliflower, in small florets
  • 250 g green beans, sliced
  • 250 g pickling onions, peeled
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 60 ml coarse salt
  • 750 ml white vinegar
  • 250 ml sugar
  • 60 ml cake flour
  • 30 ml mustard powder
  • 10 ml turmeric
  • 5 ml ground ginger

Method

  1. Sprinkle the vegetables with the salt, cover with cold water and leave overnight. Drain and rinse.
  2. Bring 625 ml of the vinegar and the sugar to the boil. Add the vegetables and cook for 5 minutes; they must stay crisp.
  3. Mix the flour, mustard powder, turmeric and ginger to a smooth paste with the rest of the vinegar.
  4. Stir the paste into the pot and cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is thick.
  5. Spoon into hot, sterilised jars and seal at once.

Nico's tip

Good with cold meat and cheese.
