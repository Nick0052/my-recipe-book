Mushroom Sauce

Creamy mushroom sauce

Serves 4 ✦ 20 min

Ingredients

  • 30 ml butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 ml dried thyme
  • 15 ml cake flour
  • 125 ml beef stock
  • 250 ml cream
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the butter until soft. Add the mushrooms and fry over high heat until browned.
  2. Add the garlic and thyme and fry for 1 minute. Stir in the flour.
  3. Add the stock and cream, stirring. Simmer for 5–8 minutes, until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.
  4. Season and serve over steak, chicken or pap.

Nico's tip

Brown the mushrooms properly before adding liquid; that is where the flavour is.
