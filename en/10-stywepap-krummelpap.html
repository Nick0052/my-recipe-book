Recipe 11

Stiff and Crumbly Pap

Stiff and crumbly maize porridge

Serves 6   ✦   40 min

Ingredients

  • Stywepap (stiff):
  • 1 litre water
  • 7 ml salt
  • 625 ml (2½ cups) white maize meal
  • Krummelpap (crumbly):
  • 500 ml water
  • 5 ml salt
  • 750 ml (3 cups) white maize meal
  • 30 ml butter

Method

  1. Stywepap: Bring the salted water to the boil in a heavy pot. Pour the maize meal into the centre in a mound. Do not stir.
  2. Put the lid on and let it cook for 5 minutes. Then stir hard with a wooden spoon until smooth.
  3. Cover and cook over very low heat for 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes.
  4. Krummelpap: Bring the salted water to the boil. Add all the maize meal at once and leave it for 2 minutes.
  5. Stir with a fork until it forms crumbs. Cover and steam over very low heat for 30–40 minutes, fluffing with the fork every 10 minutes. Stir in the butter.

Nico's tip

Pap and sauce belong at every braai.
