Recipe 9

Braai Sandwiches

Toasted cheese, tomato and onion sandwiches on the braai

Makes 6   ✦   20 min

Ingredients

  • 12 slices white bread
  • Soft butter
  • 250 g Cheddar cheese, grated or sliced
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced into rings
  • 60 ml fruit chutney
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Spread chutney on 6 slices of bread. Top with cheese, tomato and onion, and season.
  2. Close with the other slices and butter the outsides.
  3. Pack them into a hinged braai grid.
  4. Braai over gentle coals for 8–10 minutes, turning often, until golden and the cheese has melted.

Nico's tip

Braai them at the end, when the coals have died down.
