Coleslaw

Cabbage and carrot coleslaw

Serves 6–8 ✦ 20 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 carrots, grated
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 125 ml condensed milk mayonnaise (or plain mayonnaise)
  • 15 ml white vinegar
  • 5 ml sugar
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the cabbage, carrots and onion in a large bowl.
  2. Stir the vinegar and sugar into the dressing.
  3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss well. Season.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Nico's tip

Add a handful of raisins or chopped pineapple for a sweeter salad.
