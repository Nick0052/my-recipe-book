Recipe 39

Custard

Homemade vanilla custard

Serves 6 ✦ 15 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 60 ml sugar
  • 15 ml cornflour
  • 5 ml vanilla essence

Method

  1. Heat the milk until just below boiling.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks, sugar and cornflour until smooth and pale.
  3. Pour the hot milk slowly onto the yolks, whisking all the time. Pour it back into the pot.
  4. Stir over low heat for 5–8 minutes, until the custard coats the back of a spoon. Do not let it boil.
  5. Stir in the vanilla. Serve warm or cold.

Nico's tip

Press cling film onto the surface so it does not form a skin.
