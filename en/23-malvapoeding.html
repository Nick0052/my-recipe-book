Recipe 52

Malva Pudding

Baked apricot sponge pudding with cream sauce

Serves 8   ✦   1 h

Ingredients

  • 250 ml sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 15 ml apricot jam
  • 250 ml cake flour
  • 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 ml salt
  • 15 ml butter
  • 5 ml vinegar
  • 125 ml milk
  • Sauce:
  • 250 ml cream
  • 125 g butter
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 125 ml hot water

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a 25 cm oven dish.
  2. Beat the sugar and eggs until thick and pale. Beat in the jam.
  3. Sift the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt.
  4. Melt the butter and add the vinegar and milk.
  5. Fold the flour and the milk mixture into the eggs, a little at a time.
  6. Pour into the dish and bake for 40–45 minutes until golden brown.
  7. Heat the sauce ingredients until the butter melts. Pour over the pudding as soon as it comes out of the oven.

Nico's tip

Serve warm with custard or ice cream.
