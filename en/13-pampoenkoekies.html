Recipe 15

Pumpkin Fritters

Pumpkin fritters with cinnamon sugar

Makes about 16   ✦   40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml cooked, mashed pumpkin (about 600 g raw), well drained
  • 250 ml cake flour
  • 10 ml baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • A pinch of salt
  • Oil for frying
  • 125 ml sugar mixed with 10 ml ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix the pumpkin, flour, baking powder, egg and salt into a soft batter.
  2. Heat 1 cm of oil in a pan. Drop in spoonfuls of batter.
  3. Fry for 2 minutes per side until golden. Drain on paper towel.
  4. Roll in cinnamon sugar while still warm.

Nico's tip

Drain the pumpkin well, or the fritters turn heavy.
