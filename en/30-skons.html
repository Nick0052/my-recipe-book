Recipe 46

Scones

Scones with jam and cream

Makes 12 ✦ 30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml cake flour
  • 20 ml baking powder
  • 2 ml salt
  • 30 ml sugar
  • 60 g butter, cold and cubed
  • 1 egg
  • About 150 ml milk
  • Jam and whipped cream, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C and grease a baking tray.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt. Stir in the sugar, then rub in the butter until it looks like breadcrumbs.
  3. Beat the egg with the milk. Keep 15 ml aside for brushing and cut the rest into the flour with a knife to form a soft dough. Do not knead.
  4. Pat the dough out 2 cm thick on a floured surface and cut out rounds.
  5. Put them on the tray, brush the tops with the egg mixture and bake for 12–15 minutes, until risen and golden.

Nico's tip

Work the dough as little as possible for light scones.
