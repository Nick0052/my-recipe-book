Recipe 53

Jan Ellis Pudding

Baked apricot-jam pudding with butter sauce

Serves 8 ✦ 1 h

Ingredients

  • 30 ml butter, soft
  • 125 ml sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 30 ml apricot jam
  • 250 ml cake flour
  • A pinch of salt
  • 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
  • 125 ml milk
  • Sauce:
  • 250 ml cream
  • 250 ml boiling water
  • 200 ml sugar
  • 125 g butter
  • 5 ml vanilla essence

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a 25 cm oven dish.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the jam.
  3. Dissolve the bicarbonate of soda in the milk.
  4. Sift the flour and salt. Fold into the egg mixture in turns with the milk.
  5. Pour into the dish and bake for 35–40 minutes, until dark golden and firm.
  6. Heat the sauce ingredients until the butter melts and the sugar dissolves. Pour slowly over the pudding as soon as it comes out of the oven.

Nico's tip

Pour the sauce over slowly, so the pudding soaks it all up.
