Grandpa Paul's Oxtail Potjie

Grandpa Paul's oxtail potjie

Serves 6   ✦   3–4 h   ✦   Three-legged potjie or heavy casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 kg oxtail, chopped into pieces
  • 100 ml oil
  • 2 large onions, chopped or sliced into rings
  • 2–3 garlic cloves
  • 63 g (1 packet) oxtail soup powder, or brown onion soup powder
  • 500 ml beef stock (not in Oupa's list; adjust to taste)
  • 250 ml red wine (optional, for depth)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Braai or steak-and-chop seasoning
  • 15 baby potatoes, or 6 medium potatoes cut in halves
  • 15 baby carrots, cleaned
  • 250 g green beans
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 6 baby marrows (zucchini), cut into 2 cm rounds

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a three-legged potjie pot or heavy casserole over medium heat. Brown the oxtail until golden.
  2. Add the onions and garlic and sauté for 2–3 minutes until translucent.
  3. Mix the soup powder, stock and wine. Pour over the meat and bring to the boil.
  4. Put the lid on and simmer over slow coals or low heat for 2–2½ hours, until the meat starts to feel tender. Do not stir.
  5. Skim off the excess fat.
  6. Layer the vegetables on top: first the potatoes and carrots, then the beans, mushrooms and baby marrows.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and braai or steak-and-chop seasoning.
  8. Put the lid back on and simmer for another 30–45 minutes, until the vegetables are tender but not mushy.

Grandpa Paul's tip

Keep the heat low for the best result. Serve with rice, pap or fresh bread to soak up the sauce.
