Recipe 41

Vetkoek

Deep-fried bread puffs

Makes 12   ✦   1 h 45 min including rising

Ingredients

  • 500 g (4 cups) cake flour
  • 10 g instant dry yeast
  • 10 ml sugar
  • 5 ml salt
  • About 375 ml lukewarm water
  • Oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add enough water to make a soft dough.
  2. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and leave in a warm place for 1 hour, until doubled.
  3. Knock the dough down and shape it into 12 balls. Let them rise for 15 minutes.
  4. Heat the oil to 170 °C. Fry a few at a time for 3–4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towel.

Nico's tip

Fill with curried mince, or eat with apricot jam and cheese.
