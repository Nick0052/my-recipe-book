Recipe 13

Samp and Beans

Samp and sugar beans

Serves 6–8 ✦ 3 h + overnight soaking

Ingredients

  • 500 ml samp (stampmielies)
  • 250 ml dried sugar beans
  • 2 litres water, plus extra if needed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 30 ml oil or butter
  • 10 ml salt
  • Black pepper
  • 30 ml butter, to finish

Method

  1. Rinse the samp and beans. Cover with plenty of cold water and soak overnight.
  2. Drain. Put the samp and beans in a heavy pot with the 2 litres of fresh water and bring to the boil.
  3. Turn the heat down, cover and simmer for 2–2½ hours, until soft. Stir now and then and add hot water if it cooks dry. Do not add salt yet.
  4. Fry the onion in the oil until golden.
  5. Stir the onion, salt and pepper into the samp and cook uncovered for 15 minutes more, until thick and creamy. Stir in the butter.

Nico's tip

Salt only at the end, otherwise the beans stay hard.
