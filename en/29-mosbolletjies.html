Recipe 44

Aniseed Buns

Sweet aniseed buns

Makes 2 loaves ✦ 1 h + 3 h rising

Ingredients

  • 1 kg white bread flour
  • 10 g instant dry yeast
  • 200 ml sugar
  • 5 ml salt
  • 15 ml aniseed
  • 250 ml milk
  • 250 ml white grape juice
  • 125 g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 30 ml sugar dissolved in 30 ml hot water, for glazing

Method

  1. Warm the milk, grape juice and butter together until the butter melts. Cool to lukewarm and beat in the egg.
  2. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and aniseed. Add the liquid and mix to a soft dough.
  3. Knead for 10–15 minutes, until smooth and elastic. Cover and leave in a warm place for 1½–2 hours, until doubled.
  4. Grease two loaf tins. Shape the dough into balls the size of an egg and pack them tightly against each other in the tins, so they rise upwards.
  5. Cover and let them rise for 45–60 minutes, until they stand above the rim.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 35–40 minutes, until golden brown. Brush with the sugar water while still hot.

Nico's tip

Break them apart and eat them warm with butter, or dry them out for rusks.
