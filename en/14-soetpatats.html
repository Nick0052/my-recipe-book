Recipe 16

Glazed Sweet Potatoes

Sweet potatoes in cinnamon syrup

Serves 6   ✦   45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg sweet potatoes, peeled and sliced 2 cm thick
  • 60 g butter
  • 125 ml brown sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 125 ml water
  • A few strips of orange peel (optional)
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Layer the sweet potatoes in a heavy pot with the butter, sugar, cinnamon and orange peel.
  2. Add the water and salt. Cover and cook over low heat for 25 minutes.
  3. Uncover and cook for 10–15 minutes more, shaking the pot, until the syrup is thick and sticky.

Nico's tip

Don't stir, or the sweet potatoes will break.
