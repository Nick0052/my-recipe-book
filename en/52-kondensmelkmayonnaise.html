Recipe 36

Condensed Milk Mayonnaise

Condensed-milk salad dressing

Makes about 500 ml ✦ 10 min

Ingredients

  • 385 g sweetened condensed milk (1 tin)
  • 125 ml white vinegar
  • 60 ml oil
  • 10 ml mustard powder
  • 5 ml salt
  • White pepper

Method

  1. Whisk the mustard powder, salt and pepper into the vinegar.
  2. Add the condensed milk and oil and whisk until smooth and thick.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes; it thickens as it stands.
  4. Stir through potato salad or coleslaw.

Nico's tip

Thin it with a little milk if it is too thick.
